Open on both sides. Built on respect.

The BiFold is a welcoming room where bisexual and bi-curious adults socialize, connect and build community. By attending, you agree to these standards.

The six standards

i. Every identity is welcome

Treat everyone with kindness and courtesy. Different identities, orientations, relationship styles and backgrounds are welcome here. Harassment, discrimination, bullying or hateful behavior toward any guest or staff member will not be tolerated.

ii. Consent is mandatory

  • No means no.
  • Silence is not consent.
  • Consent may be withdrawn at any time.
  • Respect personal boundaries.
  • Repeated unwanted advances or coercive behavior will result in removal.

iii. Keep other guests private

Many guests value discretion.

  • Don't photograph or record attendees without permission.
  • Don't tag guests on social media without consent.
  • Don't publicly share another person's attendance.

Help us keep a space where everyone can be themselves.

iv. Zero harassment

These are prohibited:

  • Unwanted touching
  • Persistent advances after rejection
  • Sexual harassment
  • Threatening or intimidating behavior
  • Stalking
  • Photography without permission

Guests who break these standards may be removed without refund.

v. BYOB, responsibly

Enjoy yourself responsibly. Guests who become excessively intoxicated or disruptive may be asked to leave. Please arrange a safe ride home.

vi. Every body, every pairing

Our community welcomes people of many identities, orientations, relationship styles and backgrounds. Treat everyone with the respect you'd like to receive.

Right to refuse service

Hosts can say no, too.

The BiFold may refuse admission to, or remove, any guest whose behavior is disruptive, unsafe, disrespectful, or inconsistent with these standards. These decisions protect the comfort, privacy and safety of our community.

Thank you

Be welcoming. Be respectful. Be authentic. Be open-minded.

Together, we make a place where bisexual and bi-curious adults can connect, celebrate and belong.
