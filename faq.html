The honest answers.

First lifestyle party or one of many, we're glad you're here. Still unsure after reading? Ask the hosts.

Before you attend

What is The BiFold?

A boutique lifestyle community and party for bisexual and bi-curious adults — and the people who celebrate them. Our nights mix socializing, entertainment and exploration, built on respect and enthusiastic consent.

Who can attend?

Anyone 21 or older who shares our values of inclusion, consent and respect. Singles, couples, polyamorous relationships, bisexual, bi-curious and pansexual people, LGBTQ+ allies and open-minded adults all have a place here.

I'm coming alone. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many guests come solo and quickly find how welcoming the room is. Connect at your own pace.

What should I wear?

Our standing dress code always applies. Some parties add a theme — if so, it's in the event listing. Stylish self-expression is encouraged.

The experience

Are your events sexual?

Yes. Most BiFold parties include spaces where consensual sexual activity happens between guests. Taking part is never expected. Many guests just socialize, dance, flirt, watch performances or make friends — and they're just as valued.

Will I be pressured to participate?

Never. Enthusiastic consent is the base of our culture. No guest should feel pushed into conversation, affection or sexual activity. "No" is always a complete answer.

Can I come just to socialize or watch?

Absolutely. Many first-timers simply enjoy the atmosphere and meet like-minded people. Nobody is expected to do anything they don't enthusiastically choose.

What might I see?

Depending on the party, you may see consensual adult sexual activity between many kinds of partners, while other guests talk, dance, relax or enjoy the entertainment. Everyone picks their own comfort level, and every choice is respected.

Consent, safety & privacy

What is your consent policy?

Consent must be enthusiastic, informed, ongoing and freely given. It can be withdrawn at any time. Pressure is never acceptable. Respecting boundaries is everyone's job.

Is my privacy protected?

Yes. Photographing or recording guests is strictly prohibited at our parties, so everyone can relax and be themselves.

Online, registration and payment are processed securely. The BiFold does not keep sensitive payment details or extra personal information that could expose members later. We collect only what we need to run your night, and protect it — online and in person.

What if someone behaves inappropriately?

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or ignoring someone's boundaries won't be tolerated. Our staff can remove anyone whose behavior breaks our community standards.

How do you keep it welcoming?

We celebrate every orientation, identity, body type, relationship style, gender expression and experience level. First party or fiftieth, you deserve to feel welcome and respected.

Spots & parties

Can I bring a friend or partner?

Absolutely. Many guests come with partners or friends; others arrive on their own. Experience The BiFold however feels best to you.

Do I need to register in advance?

Yes — most parties need advance registration to get on the guest list. Any special requirements are listed on the event page.

Can I get a refund?

No. All donations are final and non-refundable.

Our parties have limited space, so each RSVP holds a spot someone else could have had. Once you're confirmed, we commit resources and hold that spot for you alone. Please only RSVP when you're confident you can come.

If The BiFold postpones or cancels a party, registered guests will be told about any available options.

How do I hear about new parties?

Join the guest list. Many parties have limited space and fill quickly.

Our community promise

The BiFold promise.

  1. Respect every person.
  2. Practice enthusiastic consent.
  3. Celebrate authenticity.
  4. Welcome curiosity without judgment.
  5. Build genuine connections.

Come to socialize, explore, connect, dance, watch or play — you belong here.
