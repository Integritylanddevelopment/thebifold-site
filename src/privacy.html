Your privacy matters.

Last updated: June 16, 2026

At The BiFold, your privacy matters. We believe that participating in our community should be comfortable, respectful, and discreet. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

By using our website or attending our events, you agree to the practices described below.

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you interact with our website or purchase access to an event, you may provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if voluntarily provided)
  • Billing information (processed securely through third-party payment providers)
  • Event preferences
  • Messages sent through our contact forms

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may automatically collect technical information including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device information
  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on the site
  • Referral sources
  • Cookies and similar technologies

This information helps us improve our website and user experience.

How we use your information

We use information we collect to:

  • Process event registrations and donations
  • Communicate about upcoming events
  • Respond to inquiries
  • Send newsletters or promotional emails (only if you opt in)
  • Improve our website and services
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

Your privacy at events

While we strive to create a welcoming and respectful environment, attendance at our events is public to other attendees. Participants are expected to respect one another’s privacy, and photography or recording may be restricted depending on the event.

The BiFold cannot guarantee that another attendee will not recognize or identify you, and attendance is at your own discretion.

Payment information

For your security: The BiFold does not store your credit card or payment information. Payments are processed by trusted third-party payment processors that maintain their own security and privacy standards.

Personal information security

We take commercially reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and users should understand that all online activity carries some inherent risk.

We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your information is used solely for operating our events, communicating with you, and improving our services.

Email communications

If you subscribe to our mailing list, we may send:

  • Event announcements
  • Community updates
  • Special invitations
  • Promotions

You may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to:

  • Remember preferences
  • Improve performance
  • Analyze website traffic
  • Enhance user experience

Most web browsers allow you to disable cookies if you choose.

Third-party services

Our website may use third-party providers for services such as:

  • Payment processing
  • Email marketing
  • Website analytics
  • Event registration
  • Website hosting

These providers maintain their own privacy policies governing their handling of information.

Children’s privacy

Our events and website are intended only for adults 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services
  • Maintain business records
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our policies

When information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymized when practical.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Request information about how your data is used

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Community respect & confidentiality

We understand that privacy is especially important within our community. While we work to protect the information you share with us, we also ask every attendee to respect the confidentiality and personal boundaries of others.

Harassment, unwanted disclosure of another person’s identity or attendance, or sharing photos without permission may result in removal from events and future participation restrictions.

Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through the contact form on our website, or email admin@thebifold.com.

Our commitment

At The BiFold, we believe community begins with trust. We are committed to fostering an inclusive, respectful, and discreet environment where adults can connect authentically and confidently. Protecting your privacy is an important part of that commitment, and we continually strive to handle your information with care and transparency.
