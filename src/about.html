Meet Summer & Mike.

Summer and Mike are a DFW couple. Through their long-running brand, Summer's Home for Wayward Swingers, they built a name for memorable nights and welcoming spaces for open-minded adults.

As that community grew, so did their sense of how much judgment still surrounds bi play in the lifestyle. So they built something new: a brand just for bisexual and bi‑curious adults, where people can explore with confidence, feel seen without pressure, and connect somewhere both classy and comfortable.

That became The BiFold.

Who it's for

Single, partnered, or just curious.

Experienced or brand new, you're welcome. Our parties are built for real conversation and lasting friendships — in a room where nobody has to explain who they're attracted to.
