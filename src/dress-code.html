Dress to impress. Dress to express.

Confidence is the best accessory. Elegant, edgy, playful, seductive or glam — wear what makes you feel attractive and like yourself, and helps make the night feel special.

The simple rule

Creativity is welcome. Effort is expected.

This isn't a casual hangout — it's a hosted night where every guest helps set the mood.

What to wear

Yes Recommended

  • Cocktail attire
  • Fashion-forward evening wear
  • Dress shirts & blazers
  • Tailored pants
  • Dresses & jumpsuits
  • Stylish separates
  • Leather & upscale alternative fashion
  • Lingerie-inspired fashion with tasteful layering
  • Dress shoes, boots, heels or fashion sneakers

No Not permitted

  • Gym or athletic wear
  • Sweatpants or joggers
  • Basketball or cargo shorts
  • Pajamas or robes
  • Flip-flops or house slippers
  • Dirty or heavily worn clothing
  • Undershirts worn alone
  • Offensive graphics or language
  • Excessively ripped clothing
  • Costumes (unless announced)

Fetish Alternative fashion

Welcome when it's upscale and respectful.

  • Leather
  • Latex
  • PVC
  • Harnesses
  • Corsets
  • Bodysuits
  • Fashion masks

Keep fetish wear tasteful and right for a social setting.

Lingerie Done elegantly

Lingerie-inspired fashion can absolutely be part of a stylish outfit. Stay appropriately covered in common areas. Unsure? A jacket, mesh layer, skirt or accessory is often the perfect finish.

Shoes Footwear

Shoes are required at all times unless the venue says otherwise. Pick a pair that suits your outfit and lets you move comfortably and safely all night.

Care Hygiene matters

  • Showered
  • Clean clothing
  • Oral hygiene
  • Deodorant
  • Light fragrance

A comfortable room is everyone's job.

Celebrate your style

No rules about gender.

The BiFold welcomes every gender identity, body type, style and expression. There's no expectation to dress in a traditionally masculine or feminine way. Wear what makes you feel confident while respecting the upscale night and your fellow guests.

Our philosophy

It's not about being exclusive.

It's about a room where everyone feels they've stepped into something special. If your outfit makes you feel confident, shows care, and fits the upscale mood — you're probably on the right track.
