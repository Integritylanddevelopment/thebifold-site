All questions {ARROW}

Are your events sexual?

Yes. Most BiFold parties have spaces where consenting adults play. Taking part is never expected — plenty of guests just socialize, dance or watch.

I'm coming alone. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Many guests come solo and connect at their own pace.

Is my privacy protected?

Yes. Photos and recordings of guests aren't allowed. We don't store your card details, and we only keep what we need to run the event.

Why do I have to be approved?

Every guest is reviewed so the room stays balanced, respectful and safe. It's also why the address is only sent after approval.

Can I get a refund?

No — donations are final. Spots are limited, so only book if you can come. If we cancel, we'll contact you with options.
